Our Story

A neighbourhood food store built on tradition.

Shankar Pavbhaji began with a simple promise: serve pav bhaji that tastes like home. Every plate is cooked fresh on the tawa, seasoned with aromatic house spices, and finished with a generous brush of butter. Whether you are grabbing a quick bite or ordering for a gathering, we focus on consistent quality, quick service, and a friendly welcome.
